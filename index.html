The Reality

Why Great Ideas Die

95% of startups fail, taking thousands of incredible products into oblivion.

Lack of Funding

Ran out of capital before reaching product-market fit or sustainable unit economics.

Wrong Timing

Launched too early before technology mature, or too late against entrenched giants.

Co-founder Conflicts

Misaligned vision, equity disputes, or operational friction breaking leadership.

Poor Marketing

Built a great product but failed to acquire scalable user channels cost-effectively.

Founder Burnout

Relentless pressure and exhaustion leading team to abandon promising momentum.

Pivot Failure

Pivoted too slowly or chose the wrong direction when initial thesis ran out of steam.
